# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on reproducible engineering evidence and quantitative rigor

Unacceptable behaviors include harassment, personal attacks, publishing others' private information (including live trading credentials or account data) without explicit permission, or other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately via [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/Ziqi/KLine-Kronos-Suite/security/advisories/new) or repository maintainer contact. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly.
